📜 Community Guidelines

Welcome to our community! These guidelines help keep this space safe and welcoming.

  • Be kind. Treat others with respect and empathy.
  • Privacy matters. Don't share others' content without permission.
  • No harassment. No hate speech, threats, or bullying.
  • Authenticity. Be yourself. No impersonation.
  • Healthy boundaries. It's okay to take breaks. It's okay to say no.
  • Content warnings. Tag sensitive topics when needed.
