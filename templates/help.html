Help Center

Getting Started

How do I sign up? Click "Sign Up" on the login page. Pick a username (3-30 chars), enter your email, and choose a password of at least 8 characters.

How do I find friends? Visit the Discover page to browse recently joined users or suggested friends based on mutual connections.

What can I post? Text (up to 2000 chars), links, or photos. Every post is friends-only by default — no public option. You can also set a post to "Only me."

Privacy

Who sees my posts? Only your accepted friends, unless you choose "Only me." No public posts. No algorithms.

Can I block someone? Yes. Visit their profile and you'll find block controls. Blocked users cannot see your posts or message you.

Where is my data stored? On this server only. SQLite database. No cloud. No third-party trackers.

Moderation

How does moderation work? An agent scans posts for keywords and patterns. Clean posts pass. Flagged ones go to a human review queue. Rejected posts never appear.

My post was flagged. What now? Wait for an admin to review. You'll be notified of the decision.

Account

How do I reset my password? Use the Forgot Password link on the login page.

How do I export my data? Go to Settings and click "Export Data." You'll receive a JSON file with everything.

Can I delete my account? Yes. Deactivation anonymizes your posts and hides your profile. You can reactivate by logging back in.
